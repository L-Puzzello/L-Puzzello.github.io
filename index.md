### About me

I am an Associate Professor in the [Department of Economics](https://www.monash.edu/business/economics) at the [Monash Business School, Monash University](https://www.monash.edu/business). 


My research rigorously pursues a broad set of policy-relevant questions primarily in the field of International Trade. My work has been published in the _American Economic Review_, _Journal of International Economics_, and the _Journal of Applied Econometrics_, among other leading international journals. 


I am currently working on an _Australian Research Council Discovery Project_ that explores the legal, political and economic causes and consequences of investment screening policies primarily implemented on the basis of national security concerns.  A different area of my current research focuses on accurately identifying the effect of trade policy, particularly non-tariff measures, on trade and countries’ welfare. My previous research (still very much in my mind) has focused on understanding the effects of trade on growth, volatility and the environment. 


I am a co-organiser of the Melbourne Trade Workshop and the Online Australian Seminar in International economics ([OASIS](https://sites.google.com/view/oasis2022/home)). If interested in the [OASIS](https://sites.google.com/view/oasis2022/home), please join us by registering [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfGqNVi-qfmKt0Fb75cu-NG14xM_mrrS0a1YbKniJMmVurhoQ/viewform).  


I am a PI of [SoDa Labs](https://www.monash.edu/business/impact-labs/soda-labs) at Monash University, an empirical research laboratory that applies new tools from data science to answer social science questions.
